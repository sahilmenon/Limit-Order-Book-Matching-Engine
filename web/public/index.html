Limit Order Book / matching engine

A C++20 price-time-priority matching engine compiled to WebAssembly. Switch between synthetic order flow and a recorded NASDAQ ITCH replay; both run entirely in your browser.

Two sources, one engine. Synthetic generates a stream of orders (resting quotes on both sides, plus aggressive orders that cross the spread) and matches them live. Real · AAPL replays a recorded slice of NASDAQ TotalView-ITCH, rebuilding Apple's actual book from that session. Both run entirely in WebAssembly and update every frame:

  • Ladder resting limit orders by price. Asks (sells) sit above the mid, bids (buys) below; the bar shows size relative to the deepest level. The best bid and best ask meet at the spread.
  • Tape executions as they print, newest on top. A trade fires whenever an incoming order crosses the spread and matches resting size at the maker's price.
  • Stats live top of book, the count of resting orders, cumulative matches, and how many orders the engine is processing per second. Drag Speed to push it harder.
